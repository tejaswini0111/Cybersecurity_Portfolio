01 / ABOUT

Curious by nature.
Focused on security.

A little context behind the work.

My journey in computer science has grown into a strong interest in understanding, detecting and responding to cybersecurity threats.

I have 2 years of experience in enterprise Microsoft/Azure environments, with exposure to alert triage, incident escalation, MITRE ATT&CK mapping and vulnerability remediation. I enjoy connecting security concepts to practical investigation: examining network activity, understanding suspicious indicators and building small tools that make technical findings easier to interpret.

This lab brings together projects that help me develop that mindset. I am continuing to strengthen my security operations knowledge, particularly around SIEM workflows, Microsoft Sentinel, Defender XDR and KQL, while pursuing the Microsoft SC-200 certification.
